# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions
and any other project space, is expected to be respectful and constructive.

- Assume good intent, disagree about ideas and not people, and keep feedback
  specific and kind.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and sustained disruption are not
  acceptable.
- Do not post other people's private information, credentials or confidential
  data.

Maintainers may edit, remove or decline contributions and comments that do not
meet these expectations, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem,
contact the maintainer privately through the repository's security advisory
form or the contact listed on their GitHub profile.
